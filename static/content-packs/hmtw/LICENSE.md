## What this pack contains, and what it deliberately omits

This pack carries the book's **game text only**, reused under the grant above:
"the mechanics and game text of _His Majesty the Worm_ may be reused freely.
Third-party compatible works are welcomed." No art from the book is included;
all artists retain copyright of their work.

## The card art

The card table's faces are Pamela Colman Smith's seventy-eight designs as they
were printed in **A. E. Waite, _The Pictorial Key to the Tarot_ (William Rider
& Son, 1911)** — the black-and-white line plates from Part II of that book, not
the colour deck. Published in 1911, they are in the public domain in both the
United Kingdom and the United States on publication date alone.

The plates come from Wikimedia Commons, and **the provenance is proved per
plate rather than asserted**: `art/art-manifest.json` records, for every one of
the seventy-eight, the Commons file it came from, that file's licence tag, the
crop taken from it, and the hash of what shipped. `tools/build_card_art.py`
re-fetches those tags on every run and **fails the build** if any plate is not
marked public domain, so this section cannot quietly drift away from the files
it describes.

The **card backs** are the Adherent of the Worm, which the book's own terms
allow and encourage for third-party work. That mark is Josh McCrowell's, used
under that grant; it is not public domain and is not covered by the paragraph
above.

No art from the book itself is included — see the notice at the top: all
artists retain copyright of their work.
