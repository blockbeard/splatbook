## What this pack contains, and what it deliberately omits

Everything here serves the character builder, and nothing else:

- **From Origins 5.5e** — the hack's character-creation rules, its player
  advice, and the text explaining each rule. The post's **DM advice** and
  adventure recommendations are omitted; this pack is not a GM guide.
- **From SRD 5.2.1** — the four backgrounds, nine species, four Origin feats,
  the twelve classes' core traits (of which Origins borrows four), equipment,
  the cantrip and level 1 spells of the Cleric, Druid and Wizard lists, and the
  skill and language tables. The SRD's class features, subclasses, higher-level
  spells, monsters, and magic items are omitted — a classless, level-less game
  has no use for them.

## Indicating changes

CC BY 4.0 asks that changes be indicated. The SRD material here has been
reformatted from prose into structured data for a character builder, and
narrowed to the subset listed above. The rules text itself is unaltered. Origins
5.5e's own rules are reproduced as written, minus the omissions noted above.

## Provenance

The SRD material was extracted from a community markdown conversion of SRD
5.2.1 rather than from the publisher's PDF (see
`tools/build_origins_data.py`). Where this pack and the official document
disagree, the official document is correct — please
[open an issue](https://github.com/blockbeard/splatbook/issues).
