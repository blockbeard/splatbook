# Gamemaster Content

By default, search and the contents list show only player-facing content.

To prevent any accidental spoilers the Gamemaster’s half of the book — Chapter 10 (The Worm Turns) and Appendices C, D, and E, where dungeon denizens, city creation, and the making of the underworld live — is opt-in.

Nothing there is necessarily forbidden reading. But if you’re a player, the underworld is better met in play: its monsters, treasures, and secrets are your Gamemaster’s to reveal.

If you run the game — or you’ve talked it over with your table and want to read on anyway — opt in below. The choice is remembered for this game only, and you can change it any time from the contents.
