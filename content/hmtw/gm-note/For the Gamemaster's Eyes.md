# For the Gamemaster’s Eyes

The link you followed leads into the Gamemaster’s half of the book — Chapter 10 (The Worm Turns) and Appendices C, D, and E, where dungeon denizens, city creation, and the making of the underworld live.

Nothing there is forbidden reading. But if you’re a player, the underworld is better met in play: its monsters, treasures, and secrets are your Gamemaster’s to reveal. If you run the game — or you’ve talked it over with your table and want to read on anyway — opt in below.
